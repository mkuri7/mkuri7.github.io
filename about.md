## The Beginning

I started my engineering career working with IBM mainframe systems.

I maintained mission-critical banking and insurance systems where reliability was not optional.

A system failure was not just a technical problem.

It could affect customers, businesses, and society.

My role was not simply writing code.

It was understanding problems, diagnosing failures, and protecting systems.

Problem Determination.

Diagnostic.

These words were part of my daily work.

In many ways, I was a doctor for computers.

## A New Development Style

Traditional software development looked like this:

~~~text
Planning
   ↓
Design
   ↓
Implementation
   ↓
Testing
   ↓
Release
~~~

AI-assisted development introduces a new collaboration model:

~~~text
Human Architect

        ↓

AI Design Partner

        ↓

AI Software Craftsman

        ↓

Reliable Product
~~~

The role of engineers is not becoming less important.

Instead, the focus moves toward:

- defining the right problems
- designing better systems
- making architectural decisions
- judging quality
- creating meaningful user experiences

The future of software development is not humans versus AI.

It is humans and AI working together.
